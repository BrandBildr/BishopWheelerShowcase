    Home / Heat pumps & solar
    Renewable energy

    Heat your home from the air, the ground and the sun.

    Renewable heating cuts running costs and your home's CO₂. As Worcester Accredited Installers we fit heat pumps and solar hot water to the same standard as our boilers.

    The options

    Four ways to heat more efficiently.

    Tap a system to see how it works and who it suits.

    Air to water heat pumpsThe straight swap for an old boiler.300%+ efficient

    An air to water heat pump draws heat from the outside air and comes with a built-in hot water cylinder, so it can replace a tired gas or oil boiler outright. It runs only on electricity, which makes it a strong choice off the gas grid, in refurbishments and in new builds.

    300%+efficient
    3 kWheat out per 1 kW in
    Built-inhot water cylinder

    Best for: oil and LPG homes, refurbishments, new builds and homes without mains gas.

    Ground source heat pumpsSteady warmth from 12°C underground.4 kW heat out per 1 kW in

    A metre or two down, the ground holds an almost constant 12°C whatever the weather above. A glycol-based ground source system harnesses that heat to give a constant supply of hot water and heating. It's a well-established, long-term investment.

    4 kWheat out per 1 kW in
    12°Cground temperature all year
    300%+efficient

    Best for: a well-established, long-term investment in heating your home.

    Air to air heat pumpsHeat or cool an open space in about a day.120 m² heated or cooled per unit

    Low maintenance and tidy on the wall, an air to air unit can heat or cool up to 120 m². It suits open-plan rooms, loft and basement conversions, extensions, classrooms and places of worship. The units also filter the air without drying it out, which helps allergy sufferers.

    120 m²heated or cooled per unit
    ~1 daytypical install
    500%efficient, at best

    Best for: extensions and conversions, and easing the load on an existing boiler.

    Solar hot waterLet the roof heat your water.50–70% of yearly hot water

    A well-sized solar thermal system should provide around 50–70% of a home's hot water, with the boiler topping up the rest. Worcester Greenskies panels pair with the Greenstar range of regular and system boilers, in gas or oil, for a highly efficient setup.

    50–70%of yearly hot water
    Gas or oilGreenstar boiler pairing
    Cylinderseparate hot water store

    Best for: homes with a Greenstar regular or system boiler and a separate hot water cylinder.

    Overview

    Bishop & Wheeler, as it stands today

    Heating and plumbing engineers in Ivybridge, established 2001. Gas Safe, OFTEC and Worcester Accredited. Taken from the live site at bishopandwheeler.co.uk (built by i-promote.eu, footer still © 2017). Logo, colours and fonts are pulled from the site's own files and CSS, not judged by eye.

    01 · Logo

    A classic serif wordmark. Keep it exactly as is.

    "Bishop & Wheeler Ltd" in a traditional serif, with "Heating | Plumbing | Solar" in navy beneath. It reads as established and trustworthy, which is the whole job for a trade firm. Only a 362 × 68 PNG exists, so a vector redraw is a quick win before any van or print work.

    Left: as supplied. Right: reversed out on navy, as used in the concept footer and van-style blocks.

    02 · Colour

    From the site's code

    Navy and red come straight from styles.css; ink from the logo file. The live site spreads red across the nav, tabs and buttons. The concept keeps navy as the lead and saves red for actions only. Outlined swatch is the one addition.

    03 · Type

    Montserrat stays, with a serif accent from the logo

    Montserrat

    Self-hosted on the live site in Regular and SemiBold. Keep, set heavier and tighter so headlines carry weight.

    Libre Caslon

    Add as an italic accent that echoes the logo's serif. A few words per page, never body copy.

    GAS SAFE REGISTERED

    Add a mono for registration numbers and specs. Montserrat Alternates and the Times fallback in body copy are dropped.

    04 · Illustrations & photography

    Their illustrations are the best visual asset

    Four flat service icons (boiler, tools, tap, solar panel) and a map pin. Simple, ownable and already theirs, so the concept builds the services, booking and area map around them. The photography is stock and Worcester product material, with no real team, vans or jobs. A half-day shoot of the team and the vans would lift everything.

    05 · Tone of voice

    Plain, honest and reassuring, buried in long paragraphs

    They talk about reliability, hard work and pride in the job, and they never oversell. The good lines are there but hidden, and accreditation logos are left to do the persuading.

    "no matter how small a job is it's important to get it right first time."Plumbing page · make this the spine of the brand

    Elsewhere the heating page reassures that a noisy system doesn't always need replacing, and the homepage leads with years in business and being fully insured. The concept keeps that honesty, shortens the sentences, puts the customer's problem first and makes "right first time" the line people remember.

    06 · Read

    What's working, what's holding it back

    Working

    • Trading since 2001, with a reputation built on hard work and reliability. Real trust capital.
    • Strong credentials: Gas Safe, OFTEC, Worcester Accredited, up to 10-year boiler guarantees.
    • A distinctive logo and a clear navy and red palette.
    • Genuinely broad service, from taps to heat pumps, gas and oil.
    • Good raw content on efficiency, renewables and guarantees.

    Holding it back

    • © 2017 in the footer and a dated build that feels its age on a phone.
    • No online booking. Every route ends at a generic callback form.
    • No faces: the people behind the business don't appear anywhere on the site.
    • Sliders, stock photos and manufacturer banners do the visual work.
    • Heat pumps sit behind long, dense copy.
    • Three phone numbers and two addresses across their site and Worcester profile.
    • Keyword-stuffed page titles ("Boiler Repairs Modbury, Plumbing Services Plymouth").
    07 · Concept direction

    Keep, elevate, show off

    Keep

    • The logo, untouched
    • Navy, red and charcoal from their CSS
    • Montserrat
    • Their four service illustrations and the map pin
    • Est. 2001, "right first time", their own words

    Elevate

    • Bolder type with a serif accent from the logo
    • Red reserved for actions
    • A red and navy "flow and return" pipe rule as the signature detail
    • An illustrated area map built around their pin
    • Mobile-first, booking always one tap away

    Lead with

    • Boilers first: installs, servicing and repairs pay the bills
    • Heat pumps as the growth story
    • Their 2001 heritage and "right first time" as the proof
